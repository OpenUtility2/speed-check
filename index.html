Network Documentation & Guide

1. Metrics Overview

Ping: Measures the round-trip latency of network requests. Lower ping (<30ms) is ideal for real-time collaboration, gaming, and VoIP communication.

Jitter: Measures the variability in ping over consecutive requests. High jitter signifies unstable routing and can cause packet stutter during video conference streams.

Packet Loss: The percentage of data packets that fail to reach their destination. Excessive packet loss leads to disconnects and lag spikes.
