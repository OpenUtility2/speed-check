Network Documentation & Guide

1. Understanding Your Metrics

Ping (Latency): The time it takes for data packets to make a round trip from your device to the server. Lower ping (<30ms) is crucial for real-time applications like online gaming, video calls, and live streaming.

Jitter: The variance in ping over time. High jitter indicates unstable connection routing, causing lag spikes or stuttering during voice and video communication.

Download Speed: Measures how fast data travels from the internet to your device. Essential for streaming 4K video, downloading large games, and browsing websites.

Upload Speed: Measures how fast your device sends data to the cloud. Important for video conference broadcasting, cloud backups, and online multiplayer uploads.

2. What is Mobile Operator Detection?

Our intelligent routing engine parses autonomous system numbers (ASN) and organization metadata associated with your active IP connection. When browsing via cellular data (e.g., 4G/5G LTE), the system automatically detects cellular network providers to help you benchmark mobile data performance accurately.

3. Troubleshooting Slow Internet Speeds

  • Restart your equipment: Power cycle your broadband router or mobile hotspot to clear cached routing tables.
  • Switch bands: If on Wi-Fi, toggle between 2.4GHz (longer range, lower speed) and 5GHz/6GHz (shorter range, maximum speed).
  • Reduce interference: Keep routers away from thick walls, dense concrete, and electronic appliances.
